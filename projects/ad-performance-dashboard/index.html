The business problem

Advertising data is often exported from different platforms in inconsistent formats. Teams spend time cleaning spreadsheets before they can answer simple questions about spend, revenue, ROAS, campaign performance and where budget should move next.

What I built

I built an interactive reporting application with Python, Streamlit and Plotly. The app transforms uploaded campaign data into a consistent analysis layer and presents decision-ready views through filters, KPI cards, charts and campaign tables.

  • Campaign and date-range filtering.
  • Calculated performance metrics and channel comparisons.
  • Interactive trend charts and campaign-level drill-downs.
  • Reusable data-cleaning and transformation logic.
  • Clear empty, invalid-file and missing-column states.

Application architecture

01Input

Campaign exports or prepared datasets enter through a controlled upload path.

02Normalize

Server-side Python logic standardizes dates, numeric fields and naming differences.

03Calculate

Reusable functions derive KPIs and grouped performance views.

04Present

Streamlit and Plotly provide interactive filters, summaries and visual analysis.

Reliability and data quality

  • Validate required columns before calculations run.
  • Handle blank values, numeric conversion and inconsistent date formats.
  • Keep metric calculations in one layer rather than repeating formulas in charts.
  • Use anonymized or sample data in public demonstrations.

Outcome

The build converts a repetitive reporting task into a reusable internal product. Instead of manually rebuilding charts for every report, a user can load the latest data, apply filters and move directly to the campaigns that require action.
