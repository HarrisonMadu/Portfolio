The business problem

Household-product shoppers often arrive from an advertisement with limited context and little patience. The page must explain the product quickly, make the offer easy to understand, work well on mobile and pass a clean order record into the business workflow.

What I built

This case study focuses on the customer-facing commerce layer for Buy and Use and how it connects to the wider retail operation. I designed and implemented responsive product-page structures, offer presentation, trust and delivery sections, and order-capture journeys.

  • Mobile-first product and offer hierarchy.
  • Responsive product imagery and supporting content sections.
  • Package or variation selection with clear pricing context.
  • Order forms with required-field and contact validation.
  • Delivery and payment messaging positioned at decision points.
  • Structured handoff from the order journey to operations follow-up.

Full-stack flow

01Discover

04Handoff

A structured order payload moves into the existing commerce and fulfilment workflow.

Frontend and integration work

The frontend was structured as reusable sections rather than one unmaintainable page. Form states, mobile spacing, visual hierarchy and calls to action were treated as product behaviour. Integration boundaries were designed around the existing commerce platform so the customer experience could improve without pretending every third-party service was custom-built.

Quality and delivery

  • Responsive checks across common mobile and desktop widths.
  • Clear form feedback for incomplete or invalid input.
  • Consistent offer information across the page and order step.
  • Optimized image placement and content order for faster comprehension.
  • No private customer or order data included in the public case study.

Outcome

The result is a clearer customer journey that supports conversion while producing a more structured order handoff for the team. It demonstrates how frontend product decisions and operational workflow design need to work together.
